# Privacy Policy — Hisab Master

Last updated: September 2026

## Who we are
Hisab Master ("we") is a personal expense and income tracking application.
Developer contact: **mdsohanislam9909@gmail.com**

## Data we collect
When you create an account and use the app, we store:

1. **Account information** — your name, email address, profile photo URL
   (when you sign in with Google) or email (when you sign in with
   email/password). This is handled by Firebase Authentication.
2. **Financial records you create** — transaction amounts, types,
   categories, dates and notes you enter. These are stored in Cloud Firestore,
   linked to your unique user ID.
3. **Locally cached data** — for offline use, recent data is cached on your
   device and automatically deleted when you clear app data or uninstall.

## What we do NOT do
- We do not sell, rent or share your data with advertisers or third parties.
- We do not track you across other apps or websites.
- We do not request access to your contacts, location, camera roll or files.
  Camera/gallery access is used only when you choose to set a profile photo.

## How your data is stored and protected
- Data is hosted on Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication) with security
  rules that allow each user to read and write only their own data.
- Communication between the app and servers is encrypted (TLS/HTTPS).

## Data deletion
- **You can delete any transaction** any time from inside the app.
- To delete all your data and account, contact us at the email above, or
  uninstall the app after deleting your Firebase account. Requests are
  processed within 30 days.

## Children's privacy
The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
their data.

## Changes
If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
